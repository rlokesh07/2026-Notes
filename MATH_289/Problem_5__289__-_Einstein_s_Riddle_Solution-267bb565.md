# Problem 5 (289) - Einstein's Riddle Solution

First, let's figure out the order of the houses:

- We know the Norwegian lives in the first house.
- The house next to his is the blue house, in the second spot.
- Since the green house is left of the white house, it has to be in spot 3 or 4.
- The owner of the green house drinks coffee.
- The middle house (third spot) drinks milk.
- Therefore, the 4th spot is green and the last spot (5th) is white.
- The Brit lives in the red house, so the only place for that is the center (3rd spot).
- This leaves the first house with the Norwegian as yellow and smokes Dunhill.
- His blue neighbor (2nd house) keeps horses.
- The Dane lives either in the blue or white house.
- Whichever house the Dane does not live in has beer and the smoking section.
- This leaves the Norwegian with water and his blue neighbor smokes blends.
- Since the blue neighbor smokes blends, the beer and blue master goes to spot 5.
- The tea-drinking Dane goes in spot 2.
- The German smokes Prince, so he fits only in spot 2 with Prince.
- The last spot (5th) is the Swede and keeps dogs.
- The Brit smokes Pall Mall and keeps birds.
- The only person neighboring the blends-smoking Dane who has cats is the Norwegian.
- This leaves the German as the only one left.

**Answer:** The German owns the fish.
